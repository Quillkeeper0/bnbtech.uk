Our history: built by students, made for students.

Buy in Bulk Technology Group UK, operating as BNB Tech, develops software, operating systems, network infrastructure, digital entertainment, communications and global mobile connectivity. We operate in the UK and under our US-market identity, BNB Tech US. BNB Tech US works like a subsidiary but is not a separate legal entity. Zapnix is a subsidiary of BNB Tech.

  1. 2019 to 2020

    Beginnings in gaming

    We started in 2019 as BNB, focused on online gaming. Our first product, Astro, was a playable game written in Python. We supported it until 2020, and it was our first step into software development.

  2. 2020 to 2022

    Web development and cybersecurity

    We grew into web development and cybersecurity improvements, mainly for businesses, and gained traction in that market. As generative AI changed how websites get built, we rethought our priorities and widened our ambitions.

  3. 2022 to 2023

    BNB becomes BNB Tech

    In 2022 BNB became BNB Tech, short for Buy in Bulk Technology. We built cryptocurrency management software and smaller apps, and many of them reached the end of their life in 2023.

    That year we became BNB Technology Group UK and started building infrastructure for a planned DNS service with Hurricane Electric. It is still reachable at 74.82.42.42 (IPv4) and 2001:470:20::2 (IPv6).

  4. 2024 to now

    UnityOS by BNB Tech

    In early 2024 BNB Tech US introduced UnityOS, a file-based operating system built around local file management and privacy. It keeps file operations on your device and limits unnecessary data leaving it. UnityOS 5 and earlier have been retired, and development continues on newer versions.

  5. 2025 to now

    Cybersecurity research

    We test and research weaknesses in parental-control software, web-filtering systems and network-management products. We care about responsible testing, accurate technical reporting and clear communication when we discuss potential vulnerabilities.

  6. 2025 to now

    Zapnix launches

    Zapnix launched in 2025 as a BNB Tech subsidiary. It brings entertainment, communications, document sharing and connectivity together in one growing service ecosystem.

  7. Zapnix service

    AudioJam

    AudioJam began as a feature inside UnityOS, then became a standalone music service under Zapnix, with pay-as-you-go access, monthly and yearly plans and contracted options. See AudioJam.

  8. Zapnix service

    The Doc

    The Doc is a lightweight document and communications app built on direct peer-to-peer connections. It supports messaging, document sharing, buying and selling, and trading.

  9. Mid-2026

    Global mobile data

    Zapnix expanded into global mobile data with BNESIM, our global connectivity partner, which provides the backend infrastructure for customers travelling internationally.

Projects change, older products end and new services take their place. We keep adapting, experimenting and building technology people can use.
